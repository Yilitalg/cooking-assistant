Garlic Chicken Rice Bowl
Time: 30 minutes
Ingredients: chicken thighs, rice, garlic, soy sauce, spinach
Steps: Cook rice. Sear chicken with garlic, add soy sauce, wilt spinach, serve over rice.

Lettuce and green apple salad
Time: 15 minutes
Ingredients: lettuce, green apple, candied pecans, cilantro, mayo, olive oil, lemon juice, honey.
Steps: blend the cilantro, mayo, olive oil, lemon juice and honey in a blender. Cut the lettuce, green apple and pecans. Put everything in a bowl and mix. 

Roasted potatoes
Time: 1 hour
Ingredients: potatoes, olive oil, garlic, paprika, salt, black pepper 
Steps: blend the  olive oil, garlic, paprika, salt and black pepper in a bowl. Wash and cut the potatoes. Put everything in a bowl and mix. Roast in the oven for 40 minutes on 375F. 

Honey Garlic Chicken
Time: 30 minutes
Ingredients: chicken thighs, honey, garlic, soy sauce, olive oil, black pepper
Steps: Mix the honey, garlic, soy sauce and black pepper in a bowl. Heat olive oil in a pan. Cook the chicken until golden and cooked through. Pour the sauce over the chicken and cook for a few more minutes until thickened. Serve with rice.

Creamy Tomato Pasta
Time: 25 minutes
Ingredients: pasta, cherry tomatoes, garlic, heavy cream, olive oil, parmesan cheese, salt, black pepper
Steps: Cook the pasta. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the garlic. Add the cherry tomatoes and cook until soft. Pour in the heavy cream and season with salt and black pepper. Add the pasta and mix. Sprinkle parmesan cheese on top.

Chicken and Roasted Vegetable Bowl
Time: 40 minutes
Ingredients: chicken breast, potatoes, zucchini, red bell pepper, olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, black pepper
Steps: Wash and cut the potatoes and vegetables. Cut the chicken into cubes. Mix everything with olive oil and the spices. Spread on a baking tray and roast in the oven for 30 minutes at 400F, until the chicken is cooked and the vegetables are tender.

Avocado and Cucumber Salad
Time: 15 minutes
Ingredients: avocado, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, cilantro, olive oil, lemon juice, salt
Steps: Cut the avocado, cucumber, cherry tomatoes and red onion. Chop the cilantro. Mix the olive oil, lemon juice and salt in a bowl. Add the vegetables and mix everything together.

Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry
Time: 30 minutes
Ingredients: beef strips, broccoli, garlic, soy sauce, honey, olive oil, rice
Steps: Cook the rice. Mix the soy sauce, honey and garlic in a bowl. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the beef strips. Add the broccoli and a little water. Cook until the broccoli is tender. Pour in the sauce and mix. Serve over rice.

Baked Chicken with Lemon and Potatoes
Time: 1 hour
Ingredients: chicken thighs, potatoes, lemon, garlic, olive oil, paprika, oregano, salt, black pepper
Steps: Wash and cut the potatoes. Mix the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic and spices in a bowl. Add the chicken and potatoes and mix everything together. Place in a baking dish and roast in the oven for 45–50 minutes at 400F, until the chicken is fully cooked.

Creamy Mushroom Rice
Time: 35 minutes
Ingredients: rice, mushrooms, garlic, onion, heavy cream, chicken broth, olive oil, parmesan cheese, salt, black pepper
Steps: Chop the onion, garlic and mushrooms. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the onion and garlic. Add the mushrooms and cook until soft. Add the rice and chicken broth. Simmer until the rice is cooked. Stir in the heavy cream and parmesan cheese.

Greek-Style Chicken Wrap
Time: 25 minutes
Ingredients: chicken breast, tortillas, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, lettuce, Greek yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, paprika
Steps: Cut the chicken into strips and season with olive oil and paprika. Cook in a pan until fully cooked. Mix the Greek yogurt, lemon juice and garlic to make the sauce. Cut the cucumber, tomatoes and lettuce. Fill the tortillas with chicken, vegetables and sauce. Wrap and serve.

Crispy Parmesan Potatoes
Time: 45 minutes
Ingredients: potatoes, olive oil, parmesan cheese, garlic powder, paprika, salt, black pepper
Steps: Wash and cut the potatoes into wedges. Mix the olive oil, parmesan cheese and spices in a bowl. Add the potatoes and mix until coated. Spread on a baking tray and roast in the oven for 35–40 minutes at 400F, until crispy and golden.

Chicken Pesto Pasta
Time: 30 minutes
Ingredients: pasta, chicken breast, basil pesto, cherry tomatoes, olive oil, parmesan cheese, salt, black pepper
Steps: Cook the pasta. Cut the chicken into cubes and season with salt and black pepper. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the chicken until fully cooked. Add the cherry tomatoes and cook until soft. Add the pasta and pesto and mix everything together. Sprinkle parmesan cheese on top.
